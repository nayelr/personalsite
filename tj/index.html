methodology

recall first.
precision before publishing.

Each published relationship needs one credible source tying the person to TJHSST and another confirming genuine founder or co-founder status. Early employees, hired executives, and people from other Thomas Jefferson high schools are excluded.

01

discover

School archives, founder directories, accelerators, universities, and alumni network traversal.

02

verify

Company bios, personal sites, filings, school publications, and reputable reporting.

03

preserve uncertainty

Unknown stays unknown. Weak leads and false positives are documented outside the verified table.
