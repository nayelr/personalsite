01

Our manifesto

Intelligence is only useful
when it works in reality.

A model can lead a benchmark and still fail your product. The difference lives in the particulars: your data, your users, your edge cases, and what a good answer actually means.

We believe those particulars deserve to be measured. Production AI should be chosen through evidence gathered from the work it will do.

That means testing quality alongside latency and cost. Examining the failures hidden inside an average. Using ground truth where it exists, and making uncertainty explicit where it does not.

Our work ends with something your team can use: a repeatable evaluation, a clear account of what breaks, and a reasoned decision about what comes next.

Measure carefully. Choose deliberately.

Our approach

Your product is
the testing ground.

01

Define what good means

We scope your live use cases, constraints, and acceptance criteria. Representative production data becomes the foundation of the evaluation.

02

Run comparable experiments

A custom harness runs your prompts across candidate models under the same conditions, recording quality, latency, and cost.

03

Understand the failures

We assess outputs against vetted answers, with model grading and domain expertise where appropriate. Agreement and correctness are reported distinctly.

04

Make the next decision

We recommend a model for each use case, with the evidence, trade-offs, and cost difference needed to make a considered choice.
